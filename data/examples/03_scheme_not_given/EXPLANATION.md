# Example 3: scheme not given

On 5 Oct 2026, Ramesh from Ganga Traders sends a voice note: *"Bhaiya 20 carton lo, 2 carton free milega."*

On 9 Oct, bill GT/1187 charges full price for 20 cartons of Sunrise Glucose Biscuit, with no free
goods on it.

The rate valid on 9 Oct is ₹395 (rate list v2), so the missing free goods are worth
2 × ₹395 = **₹790**.

This scheme is for this one order only. If Khata treated it as permanent and claimed free cartons on
every later order, that's one of the five mistakes that caps the score at 40.
