# Example 3: Scheme Not Given

## Scenario
On 5 Oct 2026, authorized salesman Ramesh from Ganga Traders sends a voice note:
*"Bhaiya 20 carton lo, 2 carton free milega."*
On 9 Oct 2026, bill GT/1187 shows 20 cartons of Sunrise Glucose Biscuit billed at full rate, with **no free goods line** added.

## Discrepancy
- Agreed scheme: 2 free cartons for 20 cartons ordered
- Rate in force on 9 Oct: ₹395 per carton (Rate List v2)
- **Claim amount**: 2 cartons × ₹395 = **₹790**

## Critical Rule
- The scheme is **one-time** for this order, NOT permanent.
- Marking it as permanent is a capping mistake.
