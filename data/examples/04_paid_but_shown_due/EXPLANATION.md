# Example 4: paid but shown due

On 5 Oct 2026 the owner pays Sharma Products ₹5,000 for bill SP/4380 by Google Pay (UPI ref
427918239012) and posts the screenshot in the chat.

On 10 Oct, Sharma Products sends a statement that still lists SP/4380 as ₹5,000 due.

The payment was made before the statement, so the statement is wrong. Khata asks Sharma Products
to correct it: **₹5,000**.
