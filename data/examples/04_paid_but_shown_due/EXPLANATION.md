# Example 4: Paid But Shown Due

## Scenario
On 5 Oct 2026, shop owner pays ₹5,000 via Google Pay UPI (UPI Ref: 427918239012) for bill SP/4380 from Sharma Products.
A screenshot of the UPI transaction is posted in the WhatsApp chat.
On 10 Oct 2026, Sharma Products sends an account statement dated 10 Oct listing outstanding dues, which still shows bill SP/4380 with ₹5,000 as "due".

## Discrepancy
- Bill SP/4380 was already paid on 5 Oct with verified UPI ref.
- The distributor's statement dated 10 Oct still claims it is outstanding.
- **Claim amount**: Payment amount = **₹5,000**.
